System Design



System Architecture



The Product and Sales Management System follows a modular architecture where different functional components work together to provide the required services.



Main Modules



\- User Management



\- Product Management



\- Sales Management



\- Supplier Management



\- Customer Management



\- Reporting



Role-Based Design



The system provides different interfaces and permissions for Admin, Manager, Employee, Supplier, and Customer users.



Admin



The Admin manages users, products, suppliers, customers, sales, reports, and system settings.



Manager



The Manager monitors products, sales, suppliers, employees, and business reports.



Employee



The Employee handles assigned operational tasks and has limited access to product and sales functions.



Supplier



The Supplier manages supplied products, supply requests, and delivery information.



Customer



The Customer browses products, views product details, and manages orders.



Incremental Development



The system can be developed in separate functional increments, allowing each module to be implemented, tested, and integrated gradually.
