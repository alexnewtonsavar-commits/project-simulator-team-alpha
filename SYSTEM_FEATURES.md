# System Features
 
## Product Management
 
The system allows authorized users to add, update, delete, search, and view product information.
 
## Sales Management
 
The system records sales transactions and allows authorized users to monitor sales activities.
 
## User Management
 
The administrator can manage users and assign appropriate roles and permissions.
 
## Supplier Management
 
The system allows suppliers and authorized users to manage supplied products and supply-related activities.
 
## Customer Management
 
Customers can browse available products, view product details, and manage their orders.
 
## Reports
 
The system provides reports and statistics to help users monitor sales and product performance.
 
## Role-Based Access
 
The system provides different interfaces and permissions for Admin, Manager, Employee, Supplier, and Customer users.
