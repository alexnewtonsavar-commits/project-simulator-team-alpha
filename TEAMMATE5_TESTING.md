\# # Testing Contribution

\#

\# ## Testing Overview

\#

\# Testing verifies that the Product and Sales Management System functions correctly and meets the specified requirements.

\#

\# ## Product Management Testing

\#

\# The product management module is tested to ensure that authorized users can add, update, search, and manage product information correctly.

\#

\# ## Sales Management Testing

\#

\# The sales management module is tested to ensure that the system correctly records, updates, and displays sales transactions.

\#

\# ## Supplier Management Testing

\#

\# The supplier management module is tested to ensure that supplier information and supply activities are displayed and managed correctly.

\#

\# ## User Role Testing

\#

\# The system contains five user roles: Admin, Manager, Employee, Supplier, and Customer. Each role is tested to ensure that users can access only the functions appropriate to their assigned role.

\#

\# ## Testing Approach

\#

\# Each module is tested after its corresponding increment is developed. Integration testing is performed after the increments are combined to verify that all modules work together correctly.
